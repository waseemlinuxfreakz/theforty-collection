The Origin

Two Garages, One Idea, Six Years.

Forty Collection started in a single-bay shop with two FJ40 frames in pieces on the floor. The thesis was simple. Every other restomod shop adapts an existing chassis. We thought the chassis was the wrong place to compromise.

Six years later, the chassis side of the business spun off into Rolling 40, a separate shop with its own crew, building frames for our FJ40 program and a few outside clients. The Forty Collection side stayed small on purpose. Six builds a year. One vehicle at a time. The way it should be when each truck takes over a year of someone's hands.
