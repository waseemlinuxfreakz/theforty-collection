# Forty Collection — PRODUCT.md

## Register

**Brand.** The site IS the product. At $250k+ per build, design carries the entire purchase decision before the call ever happens.

## Product Purpose

Forty Collection re-engineers vintage Toyota Land Cruiser FJ40s as low-volume, ultra-premium daily drivers. The vehicles are vintage above and modern below: original FJ40 body and proportions, sitting on a chassis Rolling 40 (same ownership) builds in-house.

Six to ten builds per year. [Inference, confirm with Larry/Ju/Pat] Each one one-of-one.

The site exists to:
1. Make the chassis story legible without it sounding like marketing copy.
2. Show the current inventory in a way that respects how much each truck costs.
3. Open commission conversations with serious buyers, not browsers.

## Users

Three buyer archetypes:

1. **The collector.** Already owns vintage cars. Knows the FJ40 platform. Wants the modern chassis without losing the silhouette. Cares about provenance and engineering specifics. Will read a long-form spec page.
2. **The discerning daily-driver buyer.** Has the money for a Defender Works or an ICON, comparison shopping. Wants something that looks like it was built one at a time, not assembled at scale. Skeptical of marketing.
3. **The custom commission.** Has color, leather, hardware preferences in mind. Wants to know the process, the timeline, the people who will build their truck.

## Voice

- Terse, work-focused, confident.
- We made it. We will tell you what we made. We will not oversell it.
- "Built one at a time." Not "premium luxury restoration."
- The chassis is the differentiator. The chassis story is the through-line.

## Strategic Principles

1. **The chassis is the protagonist.** Every other competitor (ICON, Velocity, Vintage Modern) sources or outsources the chassis. We engineer it. The site says this clearly and often.
2. **Photography over copy.** When in doubt, fewer words and bigger images.
3. **No volume signals.** No "+250 customers", no testimonial walls, no Trustpilot widgets. At this price, those cheapen the brand.
4. **Two trucks, two stories.** The current pair (sky-blue and desert-tan) gets editorial treatment. Each one is a feature, not a product card.
5. **The Rolling 40 connection is owned.** We do not whisper it. We name it.

## Anti-references

- Vintage Modern's wall-of-testimonials homepage
- Velocity's Owner's Portal / Talk to Sales infrastructure (we are not at that scale and that's the strength)
- Webflow template aesthetic (Bootstrap blue, four-font stacks, card grids)
- Generic restomod sites with hero metrics ("250+ builds", "30 years experience")
- Concours d'Elegance / heritage-museum aesthetic (we are modern, not nostalgic)

## Reflex-Reject Aesthetic Lanes (per Impeccable)

Don't go anywhere near these category traps:

- Cream + serif + heritage-luxury (every restomod site does this)
- Webflow editorial-modern with full-bleed scroll storytelling and overlapping type
- Dark navy + gold (finance reflex, ICON-adjacent territory)
- Olive/military green minimalism (ICON owns this)

## Implementation Constraint

WordPress + Elementor. Implementation must work within Elementor sections, columns, and widgets. Custom CSS is permitted. No build pipeline.
